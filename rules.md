# Wealth of Nations - Game Rules & Mechanics

## 1. Game Overview

Wealth of Nations is a game of industrial production and international trade. Players act as economic powers developing industries to produce commodities. The core loop involves trading commodities to acquire resources, developing industries to increase production capacity, and producing goods to sell or reinvest.

**Objective:** The goal is to earn the most **Victory Points (VPs)** by the end of the game.

## 2. Components & Entities

### **Board**

* 
**Grid:** Hexagonal grid.


* 
**Central Hex:** No tile or flag may ever occupy the central hex of the board.



### **Commodities & Markets**

There are 5 types of Commodities and 1 Financial Resource, each with a corresponding Market :

1. **Food (Yellow):** Produced by Farms. Traded on Food Market.
2. **Energy (Blue):** Produced by Generators. Traded on Energy Market.
3. **Labor (Red):** Produced by Academies. Traded on Labor Market.
4. **Ore (White/Gray):** Produced by Mines. Traded on Ore Market.
5. **Capital (Black):** Produced by Factories. Traded on Capital Market.
6. **Money/Finance (Purple):** Generated by Banks. Managed via Finance Market.

### **Industry Tiles**

* 
**Types:** Farm, Generator, Academy, Mine, Factory, Bank .


* 
**Dots:** Tiles contain "full dots" and "partial dots" on their edges.


* 
**Full Dots:** Produce 1 Commodity cube (or $30 for Banks) when the tile is active.


* **Partial Dots:** Semicircles on the edge of a tile. When two matching partial dots on adjacent tiles align, they form a full dot, increasing production efficiency.





### **Player Assets**

* **Flags:** Used to claim hexes and identify ownership of Industry tiles. Each player has 18 flags.


* **Money:** Used for trade and scoring. Kept hidden or visible (rules state "in view" but specific amount doesn't need to be declared).


* 
**Promissory Notes:** Loans that provide cash but subtract VPs at game end.

## 3. Setup

### **Market Setup**

* 
**Layout:** Place the 6 Markets around the board in order: Food (Yellow)  Energy (Blue)  Labor (Red)  Ore (Gray)  Capital (Black)  Finance (Purple).


* **Initial Stock:** Place one Commodity cube in each well of the "Starting Market Area" (the wells with pre-printed prices). Remaining cubes go to the Supply Area.



### **Player Setup**

1. **First Player Determination:** Deal one Industry tile per player face down (one Generator, the rest Farms). The player who reveals the Generator is the First Player .


2. 
**Flags:** Players choose a Flag design and take all 18 flags.



### **Packages (Starting Resources)**

Players begin by drafting **Packages**. There are **Industry Packages** (tiles/money) and **Commodity Packages** (cubes/money) .

**Drafting Order:**

* **3 Players:**
* Round 1 (Clockwise from First): 1 Industry Package.
* Round 2 (Counter-clockwise from Last): 1 Industry OR 1 Commodity Package.
* Round 3 (Clockwise from First): 1 Industry OR 1 Commodity Package.
* Round 4 (Counter-clockwise from Last): 1 Industry OR 1 Commodity Package .




* **4 Players:**
* Round 1 (CW): 1 Industry Package.
* Round 2 (CCW): 1 Industry OR 1 Commodity Package.
* Round 3 (CW): 1 Industry OR 1 Commodity Package .




* **5 Players:**
* *Note: Industry Package 2 is removed.*
* Round 1 (CW): 1 Industry Package.
* Round 2 (CCW): 1 Commodity Package .




* **6 Players:**
* Round 1 (CW): 1 Industry Package.
* Round 2 (CCW): 1 Commodity Package .





**Package Contents:**

*Industry Packages:*
1. 3 Farms
2. 3 Farms
3. 2 Generators, $10
4. 2 Academies, $10
5. 2 Mines
6. 2 Factories

*Commodity Packages:*
1. 9 Food, 1 Energy, $40
2. 3 Food, 2 Energy, 1 Labor, $40
3. 1 Energy, 3 Labor, $40
4. 1 Labor, 1 Ore, 1 Capital, $40
5. 3 Ore, $40
6. 1 Energy, 2 Capital, $40

**Placing Starting Industries:**

* Industry Packages must be placed immediately upon drafting.


* Must be placed in a single contiguous group.


* **Placement Rule:** Partial dots of *different* colors cannot touch. Partial dots of the *same* color or touching a blank side is allowed.


* Cannot place in the central hex.


* Place a Flag on each starting Industry.

## 4. Game Structure (Phases)

The game is played in rounds. Each round has three phases:

1. **Trade Phase** (Buy/Sell/Barter)
2. **Develop Phase** (Build/Expand)
3. **Produce Phase** (Run industries)

*First Player Marker passes to the left at the end of every round (after the Produce Phase)*.

---

## 5. Phase 1: Trade Phase

Players take turns taking **one single Trade Action** at a time. This cycles around the table until **all players pass in succession**.

### **Trade Actions**

1. **Buy 1 Commodity Cube:**
* Buy the cube in the well with the **lowest Buy Price** (closest to bottom right).


* Pay the "Buy Price" listed *beneath* the well.


* If the market is empty, buy from Supply at the "Market Empty Buy Price".




2. **Sell 1 Commodity Cube:**
* Sell into the empty well with the **highest Sell Price** (closest to top left).


* Receive the "Sell Price" listed *inside* the well.


* If the market is full, sell to Supply at the "Market Full Sell Price".




3. **Barter:**
* Trade commodities, money, or Promissory notes with *one* other player.


* Cannot trade Flags or Industry Tiles.




4. **Pass:** Do nothing. A player can re-join trading if the phase hasn't ended.



### **Promissory Notes (Loans)**

* Can be taken or repaid at **any time** during the Trade Phase (does not cost an action).


* 
**Taking a Loan:** Receive cash equal to **$20 minus the number of notes you already hold**.


* 
*Example:* 1st note = $20, 2nd note = $19, 3rd note = $18.




* 
**Repaying a Loan:** Cost is always **$25** to return one note to the supply.



---

## 6. Phase 2: Develop Phase

Players take turns taking **one Develop Action** at a time until all pass in succession .

### **Develop Actions**

1. **Place a Flag:**
* 
**Cost:** 1 Labor Cube.


* 
**Rule:** Must be placed in an empty hex adjacent to a hex already containing one of your Flags.


* Cannot place in the central hex.




2. **Build an Industry Tile:**
* 
**Cost:** Resource cost varies by tile (see Table below) + requires a Flag already in the hex.


* **Placement Rule:** Must not cause partial dots of *different* colors to touch. (Same colors or blank sides are allowed) .


* 
**Force Placement:** Spend **1 Capital Cube** to ignore placement rules (mismatched dots) for that tile.




3. **Move Industry Tiles:**
* 
**Cost:** 1 Capital Cube.


* 
**Effect:** Move up to 3 of your existing Industry tiles to any empty hexes or hexes containing your Flag.


* Moved tiles keep their Flags. If moved to a hex that already had a flag, the extra flag returns to supply.


* You may Force Placement during a move (costs 1 Capital total for the action, covering the move and the forced placement).




4. **Automate a Bloc:**
* 
**Cost:** 1 Energy + 2 Capital Cubes.


* 
**Effect:** Place an Automation Token on a "Bloc" (contiguous group of same-type industries).


* 
**Benefit:** The entire Bloc can now be fed with **1 Ore** instead of (1 Food per tile).




5. 
**Pass:** Do nothing.



### **Industry Costs**

| Tile Type | Build Cost (Resources) |
| --- | --- |
| **Farm** | 1 Ore, 1 Capital 

 |
| **Generator** | 1 Ore, 1 Capital 

 |
| **Academy** | 1 Ore, 1 Capital 

 |
| **Mine** | 1 Labor, 1 Energy, 1 Capital 

 |
| **Factory** | 1 Labor, 2 Ore 

 |
| **Bank** | 1 Energy, 1 Ore, 1 Capital 

 |

*Note: The Reference card usually lists "Flag" as part of the cost, but mechanically, the Flag must be placed as a separate prior action costing 1 Labor.*

## 7. Phase 3: Produce Phase

All players run their industries simultaneously (or in turn order). Players choose which "Industrial Blocs" to run. A Bloc is a group of same-type industries owned by the same player connected by shared partial dots.

### **Running a Bloc**

To produce commodities, a Bloc must be **Fed** and **Powered**.

1. **Feeding (Input):**
* 
**Standard Cost:** 1 Food per Tile in the Bloc.


* 
**Automated Cost:** 1 Ore for the *entire* Bloc (regardless of size).


* 
*Exception:* Farms feed themselves for free.




2. **Powering (Energy):**
* 
**Cost:** 1 Energy cube for the *entire* Bloc.


* 
*Exception:* Generators power themselves for free.





### **Production (Output)**

* **Yield:** Count the number of **Full Dots** in the active Bloc.
* Full Dot = 1 Commodity Cube of that industry's type.


* 
**Banks:** Produce $30 per Full Dot.




* 
**Shared Dots:** If a dot is formed by tiles owned by *two different players*, that dot does **not** produce for either player.


* **Partial Feeding:** If a player cannot feed every tile in a Bloc, they may feed only specific tiles. Only dots on fed tiles produce.



---

## 8. Game End & Scoring

### **End Conditions**

The end of the game is triggered if **any** of the following occur during the **Develop Phase** :

1. A player has placed all 18 Flags.
2. Every hex on the board is occupied (by Flag or Industry).
3. 5 out of 6 Industry stacks are empty.

### **Final Sequence**

If a condition is met:

1. Complete the current Develop Phase.
2. Play one final **Produce Phase**.


3. Play one final **Trade Phase** (players sell off remaining stock).



### **Scoring (Victory Points)**

* 
**Industries:** +4 VPs per Industry Tile on the board.


* 
**Money:** +1 VP per $10 held.


* 
**Debt:** -3 VPs per unpaid Promissory Note.



**Tie Breaker:**

1. Most Money.
2. Most Flags on board.
3. Shared victory .


## 9. Tile Types 

**Tile Quantities:**
- Farm: 15 tiles
- Generator: 9 tiles
- Academy: 9 tiles
- Mine: 9 tiles
- Factory: 9 tiles
- Bank: 9 tiles

We will describe the tiles in the game as follows:

Assuming the tile is oriented with a flat side up, the top flat side will be side 1 and the top right corner will be corner 1.

1. Farm Tile:
* One full dot in center.
* One half dot on each flat side.

2. Generator Tile:
* One full dot in center.
* One half dot on each odd-numbered flat side.

3. Academy Tile:
* One full dot in center.
* Half dots on sides 3 and 6.
* 1/3 dots on corners 2 and 6.

4. Mine Tile:
* One full dot in center.
* One half dot sides 1 and 4.

5. Factory Tile:
* One full dot in center.
* 1/3 dots on corners 3, 4, and 6.

6. Bank Tile:
* No dot in center.
* Half dots on sides 2 and 3.
